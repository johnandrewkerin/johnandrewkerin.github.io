**PhD Candidate in Mathematics**  
The Graduate Center, City University of New York

I am a PhD candidate in mathematics at the Graduate Center, CUNY.
My research interests are in differential geometry and geometric
analysis, particularly Riemannian geometry and Einstein manifolds.

[Curriculum Vitae (PDF)](/files/CV.pdf)

## Research

I am interested in the relationship between curvature and topology
in Riemannian geometry. My current research focuses on Einstein
manifolds, curvature pinching, and geometric methods for studying
topological invariants.

More broadly, I am interested in geometric analysis and
conformal geometry.
