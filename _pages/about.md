I am a PhD Candidate in Mathematics at the CUNY Graduate Center. My advisor is [Renato Bettiol](https://www.lehman.edu/faculty/rbettiol/). I teach at Hunter College. 

My research interests are in Riemannian geometry and aeometric analysis, particularly the relationship between curvature and topology. My current work uses Weitzenböck formulae and the Chern--Gauss--Bonnet theorem to investigate the geometry and topology of Einstein manifolds under various types of curvature pinching.
