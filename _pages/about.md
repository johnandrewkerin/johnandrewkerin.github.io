I am a PhD Candidate in Mathematics at the CUNY Graduate Center. I teach at Hunter College. 

My research interests are in Riemannian Geometry and Geometric Analysis, particularly the relationship between curvature and topology. My current work uses Weitzenbock formulae and the Chern--Gauss--Bonnet theorem to investigate the geometry and topology of Einstein manifolds under various types of curvature pinching.
